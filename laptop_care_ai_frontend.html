ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

How the Expert System Works

Built for the Foundation of Artificial Intelligence course (Course Code: 2ADPC204).

1

Forward Chaining (Data-Driven)

The system collects facts from your answers to simple yes/no questions. It matches these collected facts against our knowledge base of 11 IF-THEN rules to find all potential matching problems.

IF not_starting AND charger_working
THEN Battery problem
2

Backward Chaining (Goal-Driven)

To eliminate false positives, the inference engine takes every candidate conclusion as a goal and verifies backward whether all necessary supporting facts exist in the user's session data.

GOAL: Battery problem?
CHECK: Do user facts contain all rule conditions? -> Confirmed
